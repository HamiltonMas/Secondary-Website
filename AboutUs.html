Our Legacy of Excellence

Since our founding, we have been dedicated to guiding students from their very first years of learning in Elementary School, through the exciting challenges of Middle School, and into the academic and personal growth of High School.

Each stage of education is carefully designed to build confidence, character, and knowledge. Our elementary program fosters curiosity and foundational skills. Middle school encourages exploration and independence. High school prepares students for college, careers, and meaningful contributions to society.

Our History

Key milestones in our journey of nurturing young learners.

1985 - Our Beginning

Our school was founded with just a single Elementary School class, welcoming young learners into a supportive and nurturing environment.

1995 - Middle School Opens

With growing enrollment, we expanded to include a dedicated Middle School, giving students new opportunities to explore subjects and build independence.

2005 - High School Established

The addition of our High School marked a major milestone, preparing students for college and future careers with advanced academics and extracurricular programs.

2015 - Campus Growth

Expanded facilities with new classrooms, labs, and sports grounds to support growing enrollment and diverse student interests.

2023 - Today

Today, we are proud to serve hundreds of students across Elementary, Middle, and High School, continuing our legacy of excellence in education.
