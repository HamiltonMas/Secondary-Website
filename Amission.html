Admission Process

Our admission process is designed to welcome students and families into our Elementary, Middle, and High School community. Follow these simple steps to apply for the upcoming school year.

1

Inquiry & School Tour

Begin by contacting our admissions office. Families are encouraged to schedule a school tour to experience our learning environment and meet our teachers.

2

Application Form

Collect an application form from the school office. Submit the completed form along with required documents such as recent report cards, birth certificate, and teacher recommendations physically at the school.

3

Review by Committee

The admissions team carefully reviews each application, considering academic progress, social readiness, and alignment with our school’s values.

4

Admission Decision

Families will receive an official admission decision by email or phone. Accepted students will also be given details for the next steps.

5

Enrollment & Orientation

Confirm your child’s spot by submitting the enrollment agreement and deposit at the school office. Families are invited to attend an orientation to get ready for the school year.

Application Deadline

January 15, 2024

Submit your application by this date to be considered for the upcoming academic year. Late applications may be considered based on availability.
